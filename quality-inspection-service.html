Socks Quality Inspection Service

IN-PROCESS CONTROL

Inspection Is Part of Manufacturing—not a Final Add-on

For OEM and ODM orders produced by Rongxing, quality requirements are reviewed before production and checked throughout the manufacturing process. Buyers can share product specifications, approved samples and inspection requirements with our team.

  • Yarn and incoming material review
  • Knitting construction and pattern checks
  • Toe linking and workmanship inspection
  • Boarding, sizing and shape confirmation
  • Final random inspection
  • Pre-shipment packing and quantity verification

Quality Control Across the Production Cycle

Incoming Yarn

03

Toe Linking

04

Boarding

05

Final Inspection

06

Pre-shipment
