COMMON QUESTIONS

OEM/ODM Sock Production FAQ

What is your standard MOQ?

Our standard MOQ is 3,000 pairs per color and style. Requirements can vary by yarn, construction and packaging.

How long does sampling take?

Regular sampling takes approximately 7–10 working days. Complex construction or special materials may take 10–15 working days.

Can you manufacture from a reference sample?

Yes. We can evaluate physical samples, artwork or tech packs and confirm feasible construction and material options.

Do you provide private-label packaging?

Yes. Custom labels, hangtags and packaging can be included in the production program.
